# Pactra brand direction

## Positioning

**Name:** Pactra. **Tagline:** Your terms. Negotiated.

**One-line description:** Your AI negotiates. You choose the deal.

The visual identity should communicate agency, competition, and a clear agreement. The memorable screen is the live market: three distinct seller lanes converging on a buyer-approved offer.

## Personality and copy

Confident, clear, energetic, and commercially grounded. Use short action verbs: compare, counter, revise, choose, approve. Avoid robot mascots, fake claims of sentience, and exaggerated saving guarantees.

Example copy:

- Start: "What would a good deal look like?"
- Brief: "These are your limits. Your agent will work within them."
- Progress: "Studio Stack is revising the bundle."
- Recommendation: "Better video, fewer extras, within your budget."
- No deal: "No seller can meet all these requirements."
- Payment: "Review your agreement, then approve with PayPal."
- Disclosure: "Simulated sellers. Fictional products. PayPal sandbox payment."

## Visual direction

Use a light neutral canvas for legibility with an ink navigation band, violet primary actions, and restrained teal confirmations. Seller identities can use small different accent markers, but text labels must carry meaning independently.

| Token | Proposed color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas | `#F6F7FB` | Main application background |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and offer sheets |
| Ink | `#111827` | Primary text and navigation |
| Muted text | `#4B5563` | Secondary copy |
| Primary | `#5B3FD6` | Main action and buyer agent accent |
| Confirmation | `#087F6D` | Successful validations and completed payment labels |
| Attention | `#9A5A00` | Pending or caution labels |
| Error | `#B42318` | Failed constraints and blocking errors |
| Border | `#D7DCE5` | Card divisions and input boundaries |

Verify actual foreground/background contrast during implementation, including hover, focus, disabled, and compact status states. Color values are a direction, not an accessibility certification.

Start with a readable system sans-serif stack. Set monetary totals in tabular numerals. The visual hierarchy is brief constraints first, seller offers second, action timeline third. Keep prices stable in place when offers update so the user can track changes.

## Mark concept to explore later

A compact original symbol showing two opposing offers joining into one agreement, paired with a simple Pactra wordmark. Explore this as a vector design; a final logo is outside the current planning deliverable.

## Motion

Animate offer replacement gently, highlight changed fields briefly, and keep an explicit round label. Use real progress events instead of simulated typing. Honor reduced-motion preferences. Avoid a chaotic terminal aesthetic that makes money and terms difficult to inspect.
