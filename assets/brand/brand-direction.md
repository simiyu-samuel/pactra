# Pactra brand direction

Approved direction: warm editorial marketplace, October 20, 2026.

**Name:** Pactra. **Tagline:** Your terms. Negotiated.

Pactra invites people to discover products, compare choices and negotiate a fictional creator kit with native sellers. The shopping experience leads with useful imagery and readable prices. The agent is a clearly explained shopping capability.

## Visual system

| Token | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas | `#F8F6F0` | Warm ivory page background |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Forms, products and agreements |
| Ink | `#25332B` | Navigation, headings, primary actions |
| Orange | `#C74925` | Search, selected states and editorial accents |
| Muted | `#657354` | Supporting shopping evidence |
| Border | `#E3E4D8` | Subtle divisions |

DM Sans and Manrope provide the existing readable sans-serif foundation. Georgia italic adds an editorial voice to large campaign headings. Prices use tabular numerals. Use generous whitespace, strong product photographs and restrained asymmetrical curves. Shared navigation keeps Discover, Flights and Your shopping agent visible on small screens.

The existing Handshake icon supplies the brand tile. Native CSS and the established Lucide icon set provide interface marks; no third-party brand logo pack is bundled.

## Imagery and honest shopping copy

Seven original images were generated with the built-in image generator and delivered as WebP. Prompts, saved paths and uses are recorded in [image-prompts.json](image-prompts.json). Creator and coastal scenes are aspirational campaign artwork. Five equipment concepts illustrate fictional native catalog items; visible labels and detail copy distinguish these from exact product photographs. Channel3 retailer photos continue to come from provider URLs.

Use concrete shopping verbs: discover, compare, revise, choose, approve. Preserve disclosures about fictional native goods, sandbox payments, external checkout and test flight fares. Do not claim guaranteed savings, negotiation with external merchants, ticket booking or real availability from test data.

## Interaction and motion

Keep controls large enough to use, preserve keyboard focus outlines and native modal semantics, and honor reduced-motion preferences. Status text must carry meaning independently of color. Show actual room events and payment evidence. Prices and accepted terms remain readable throughout negotiation and checkout.
